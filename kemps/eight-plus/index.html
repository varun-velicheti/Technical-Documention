Understanding pH

What makes wateralkaline?

pH measures how acidic or basic a liquid is, on a scale from 0 to 14. Typical drinking water sits around pH 7; alkaline water is commonly pH 8 to 9.

pH alone doesn't make water alkaline — alkaline minerals matter too. That's why Eight Plus pairs a pH above 8 with calcium, magnesium, potassium and sodium.
