Our legacy

North Karnataka'sown water,since 1997.

Kempannavar Industries began in Hosapete with a single promise: clean, safe packaged drinking water under the name KEMP'S — the first such brand in North Karnataka.

Nearly three decades on, the same family runs a sophisticated bottling facility, an alkaline water brand and a growing range of sodas — all made by a team of engineers, technicians and quality controllers committed to superior quality.

How we work

Made with carein Hosapete.

Our sophisticated bottling facility in Hosapete lets us meet growing demand — including urgent, large-volume orders — without compromising on quality.
