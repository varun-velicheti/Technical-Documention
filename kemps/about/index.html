About us

A Hosapete storysince 1997.

Two companies, one family, and nearly three decades of bottling water and beverages for North Karnataka.

Kempannavar Industries

Where itall began.

Kempannavar Industries was established in 1997 under the ownership of K S Manjunath. The company began by manufacturing packaged drinking water under the brand name KEMP'S — the first such brand in North Karnataka.

In 2003 the company diversified into the beverage industry with its own KEMP'S brand. The plant is located in Hosapete, Ballari district, Karnataka, where sophisticated water bottling facilities let us meet the growing demands of customers across the market — including urgent requests for larger quantities.

Our team of engineers, technicians, quality controllers, sales personnel and analysts is committed to supplying superior quality products, supported by a wide network of marketing agents.

Kemp's Pet Industries

Growingthe family.

Founded by K M Sunitha in 2006, who is currently its CEO, Kemp's Pet Industries brought club soda and jeera soda into the range.

Today the range includes flavours such as lemon ginger, nannari, pudina and kokum jeera. Research and development drives the company forward, and Kemp's is proud to empower women from rural areas.
