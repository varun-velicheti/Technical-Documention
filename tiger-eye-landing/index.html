Description

Tiger eye is a natural quartz with fine, silky fibres inside it. Once polished into a bead, those fibres catch the light in a single bright band that moves as you turn your wrist.

It has long been worn as a stone of confidence and focus. Because every bead is cut from natural stone, the pattern on your bracelet will be one of a kind.

The stone

Tiger eye forms over millions of years as quartz slowly replaces fine mineral fibres, keeping their silky structure. That is what gives the stone its moving band of light, which gemmologists call chatoyancy.

We sort our beads by hand for a clear golden band and even colour, polish them smooth, and string each bracelet on a doubled cord so it holds its shape.

Many low-cost “tiger eye” bracelets are dyed glass. Real stone feels cool and heavy in the hand, and the band moves when you tilt it.
