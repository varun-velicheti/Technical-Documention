The Stone

Light, folded into quartz.

Tiger eye forms when quartz slowly replaces fine mineral fibres and keeps their silky structure. Polish it into a sphere and those fibres catch the light in a single bright band. Gemmologists call it chatoyancy, from the French for “cat’s eye.” Turn your wrist and the band moves with you.

What it stands for

Worn for centuries as a stone of resolve.

Soldiers, traders and travellers carried tiger eye as a talisman. Today people wear it as a daily reminder of who they mean to be.

Confidence

Traditionally linked with self-belief and nerve, the qualities it was carried for on long journeys.

Focus

A steady, warm stone for long days and clear decisions. Many wear it to work as a quiet anchor.

Calm

Earthy browns and gold that sit close to the skin, long said to bring balance and grounding.

Success

In many traditions it is a stone of prosperity, worn by people starting something new.

These meanings come from tradition and folklore. Tiger eye is jewellery and is not a substitute for medical or professional advice.

Yes. Every bead is natural tiger eye quartz. Because it is a natural stone, each bead has its own pattern and no two bracelets look exactly alike.

Will it fit my wrist?

The bracelet stretches to fit wrists from 17 to 21 cm, which covers most adults. If your wrist is outside that range, mention your size when you order and we will string one to fit.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available across India. You can also pay by UPI when you place your order.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 24 hours. Most metro cities receive them in 3 to 5 days, and other locations in 5 to 7 days.

What if I don’t like it?

Message us within 7 days of delivery and we will arrange a return. The bracelet should be unworn and in its box.

How do I care for it?

Take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise, and keep it away from perfume. Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Roll it on and off instead of pulling the cord.
