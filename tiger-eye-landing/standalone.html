The Stone

Light, folded into quartz.

Tiger eye forms when quartz slowly replaces fine mineral fibres, keeping their silky structure. Polish it into a sphere and those fibres catch light in a single bright band. Gemmologists call it chatoyancy, from the French for “cat’s eye.” Turn your wrist and the band glides across the bead.

What it stands for

Worn for centuries as a stone of resolve.

Soldiers, traders and travellers have carried tiger eye as a talisman. Today people wear it as a daily reminder of the person they mean to be.

i.

Focus

A steady, warm stone for long days and clear decisions. Many wear it to work as a quiet anchor.

ii.

Courage

Traditionally linked with confidence and nerve, the qualities it was carried for on long journeys.

iii.

Grounding

Earthy browns and gold that sit close to the skin, said to bring calm and balance.

These meanings come from tradition and folklore. Tiger eye is jewellery, and is not a substitute for medical or professional advice.

Questions

Before you order.

Is this real tiger eye?

Yes. Every bead is natural tiger eye quartz. Because it is a natural stone, each bead has its own pattern and no two bracelets look exactly alike.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available across India. You can also pay by UPI when you place your order.

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 24 hours. Most metro cities receive them in 3 to 5 days, and other locations in 5 to 7 days.

What if it does not fit?

Message us within 7 days of delivery and we will exchange the size or arrange a return. The bracelet should be unworn and in its box.

Can men and women both wear it?

Yes. The 8 mm bead suits a finer, everyday look. The 10 mm bead is bolder and is popular with men.

How do I care for it?

Take it off before bathing, swimming or exercise, and keep it away from perfume. Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Roll it on and off instead of pulling the cord.
