Born on an estate, raised in the city.

First Sip started with one question on a misty morning in Coorg: why does the best coffee grown in India rarely reach an Indian cup?

A note from our founder

“I grew up between coffee bushes. I wanted the city to taste what I tasted as a child: the first sip of something real.”

Ananya Rao is a third-generation coffee grower and a licensed Q-grader. After six years roasting in Melbourne and Copenhagen she came home with one goal: build India's most loved coffee house, using only Indian coffee.

Ananya Rao
Founder & Head Roaster
Our journey

From one roaster to a national brand.

2019

The family estate

Ananya returns to the Rao family estate in Coorg and starts roasting micro-lots in a 1 kg sample roaster in the drying shed.

2022

Direct-trade network

Partnerships with 40 farms in Chikmagalur, Araku and Wayanad. Farmers are paid on average 35% above the commodity price.

2024

First café opens in Bandra

The Carter Road flagship opens with a roastery bar. The saffron cortado sells out on day one.

2025

Bengaluru, Delhi and online

New cafés in Indiranagar and Khan Market, and the online roastery launches with subscriptions across India.

2026

Building the national roadmap

A new Coorg roastery, the First Sip app, and a plan for 150 cafés across 20 cities. See the plan →

What we stand for

Three promises in every cup.

These shape how we buy, roast, hire and design. When a decision is hard, we come back to these three.

100% Indian coffee

Every bean comes from an Indian estate we have visited in person. We publish the farm, altitude and harvest date on every bag.

Fair to the farmer

Multi-year contracts, quality premiums and a share of café profits returned to our farming communities through the First Sip Fund.

Care in the details

Hand-thrown cups, calibrated grinders and trained baristas. Small things, done properly, every single time.

Sustainability

Good coffee should leave the world better.
