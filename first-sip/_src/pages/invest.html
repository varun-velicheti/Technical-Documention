Why now

India is becoming a coffee nation.

Urban India is trading up from instant coffee to specialty, led by consumers under 35. Domestic coffee consumption is growing quickly, yet premium café penetration remains far below Asian peers.

{{icon:chart}}

Fast-growing demand

Café visits, specialty bean sales and at-home brewing gear are all rising year on year in metro and tier-1 cities.

{{icon:users}}

A young, premium audience

Rising disposable income and global exposure. Coffee has become a daily ritual and a social space.

{{icon:globe}}

World-class supply at home

India is a top-ten coffee producer, but most premium lots are exported. We keep the best for Indian customers.

{{icon:sparkle}}

Whitespace for an Indian icon

The premium segment is led by international chains. There is room for a proudly Indian brand with world-class standards.
