Bean to cup

Four steps. No shortcuts.

We control every stage, from the estates we partner with to the barista who hands you your cup. That is how every First Sip tastes the same in Bandra and in Indiranagar.

01 · Grown{{icon:leaf}}

Shade-grown estates

Arabica grown under silver oak and pepper vines at 1,000 to 1,500 m in the Western and Eastern Ghats.

02 · Roasted{{icon:flame}}

Small-batch roasting

12 kg batches on our Coorg roaster, profiled for each lot and rested for 7 days before brewing.

03 · Brewed{{icon:cup}}

Dialled in daily

Every café calibrates grind, dose and yield each morning. Espresso is pulled at 93°C, 18 g in, 36 g out.

04 · Served{{icon:hand}}

Poured with care

Trained baristas, hand-thrown ceramics and a space designed for you to slow down and enjoy it.
