Born on an estate, raised in the city.

First Sip started with one question on a misty morning in Coorg: why does the best coffee grown in India rarely reach an Indian cup?

Meet the founder

“I spent years writing code. Then I went looking for the perfect cup, and found it in India's own hills.”

Varun Velicheti built his career in information technology. Coffee was the ritual behind every late night and every product launch, and over time it became an obsession.

So he went to the source. Varun travelled across India's coffee country: Coorg and Chikmagalur in Karnataka, Wayanad in Kerala, the Nilgiris in Tamil Nadu, the Araku Valley in Andhra Pradesh and the young farms of the Northeast. He walked the estates at harvest, sat with growers and tasted lot after lot to find beans worth building a brand on.

First Sip brings both worlds together: the warmth of a great coffee house and the precision of a technology company, from roast profiles to the app in your pocket.

Our journey

From a home kitchen to a national brand.

2019

Code by day, coffee by night

While working in IT, Varun starts brewing, home-roasting and tasting beans from every corner of India.

2021

The coffee road trip

Varun travels through Coorg, Chikmagalur, Wayanad, the Nilgiris, Araku Valley and the Northeast, meeting growers and tasting their best lots at the source.

2022

Direct-trade network

Partnerships with 40 farms in Chikmagalur, Araku and Wayanad. Farmers are paid on average 35% above the commodity price.

2024

First café opens in Bandra

The Carter Road flagship opens with a roastery bar. The saffron cortado sells out on day one.

2025

Bengaluru, Delhi and online

New cafés in Indiranagar and Khan Market, and the online roastery launches with subscriptions across India.

2026

Building the national roadmap

A new Coorg roastery, the First Sip app, and a plan for 150 cafés across 20 cities. Become a partner →

What we stand for

Three promises in every cup.

These shape how we buy, roast, hire and design. When a decision is hard, we come back to these three.

100% Indian coffee

Every bean comes from an Indian estate we have visited in person. We publish the farm, altitude and harvest date on every bag.

Fair to the farmer

Multi-year contracts, quality premiums and a share of café profits returned to our farming communities through the First Sip Fund.

Care in the details

Hand-thrown cups, calibrated grinders and trained baristas. Small things, done properly, every single time.

Sustainability

Good coffee should leave the world better.
