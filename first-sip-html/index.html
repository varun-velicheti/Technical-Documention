01Roasted

Beans from our own estates

Shade-grown arabica from Coorg, Chikmagalur and Araku, roasted in 12 kg batches and rested for seven days so the flavour settles.

1,000–1,500 m altitudeHand-pickedSmall-batch roast
02Ground

Ground fresh, seconds before brewing

Conical steel burrs grind each dose to order. Freshly ground coffee keeps the aromas that pre-ground coffee loses within minutes.

18 g doseCalibrated daily
03Brewed

Slow-brewed with water at 93°C

We bloom the grounds for 40 seconds, then pour in slow spirals. Filtered, mineral-balanced water lets the coffee taste of its origin.
